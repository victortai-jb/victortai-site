Six things to check before you sign for a factory or warehouse

Power supply
List the machines and their load first. A unit with too little supply means an upgrade application, and that costs both money and weeks.
Floor loading
Racking, heavy machines and stacked pallets all put weight on the slab. Ask what the floor was designed to carry.
Clear height
Height decides how many racking levels fit and whether an overhead crane is possible.
Loading and access
Check the loading bay, the door size, and whether a 40-foot container can turn into the compound.
Zoning and licence
The industrial category of the land has to match your activity. Confirm with the local council before signing.
Location
Measure the drive to your customers, suppliers, workers, the ports and the two road links to Singapore.

Compare the full cost, not only the rent

Two units with the same monthly rent can cost very different amounts to occupy. Before choosing, add up the rent, the security and utility deposits, stamp duty on the tenancy, renovation and power upgrades, and the cost of reinstating the unit when you leave. I put these in one table for every unit on your short list.
