About Victor

The person behind the numbers.

Victor Tai (Tai Sing Thong, REN 26739) is a Johor Bahru real estate negotiator and Associate Leader at The Roof Realty (TRR), focusing on commercial and industrial property: shoplots, factories and warehouses. A former external auditor at Big 4 firm Ernst & Young (EY), he explains property through numbers (rent, tax, loan and yield) and matches businesses to the space they actually need. He has been a TRR Multi Million Agent every year since 2023. He is co-founder of M-Fighters, the largest team in TRR nationwide as of 2026, and founder of The Worthy Group, which produces over RM10 million in sales commission a year. He shares JB property content on Facebook, Instagram, Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Douyin and YouTube.
