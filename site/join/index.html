Join The Worthy Group

A property sales team in Johor Bahru that runs on systems

The Worthy Group (TWG) is a sales team within The Roof Realty in Johor Bahru, founded by Victor Tai (Tai Sing Thong), and produces over RM10 million in sales commission a year. TWG is part of M-Fighters, the largest team in TRR nationwide as of 2026. What sets it apart is how well the team's systems support each agent.

What the team gives you

Shared Listing Hub

One team database of listings and buyer requirements, with matching between the two. A new agent starts with inventory to work on.

Team dashboard

Activity and results are tracked in one place, so coaching is based on your numbers.

Commercial and industrial learning route

We learn shoplots, factories and warehouses together and turn what we learn into training material.

AI in daily work

Internal workshops on using AI for listings, follow-up and content.

High support, high accountability

The structure is simple: Victor works with leaders, leaders work with agents. Support and accountability come together. You get help, and you are asked about your numbers.

This suits you if

  • You want to be measured by numbers and coached on them.
  • You are ready to follow a system before inventing your own.
  • You want to learn commercial and industrial, beyond residential.

This does not suit you if

  • You are looking for a fixed monthly salary. Agency work is commission-based.
  • You prefer to work without reporting or accountability.
  • You expect leads to be handed over without doing your own prospecting.

How to apply

  1. WhatsApp Victor with a short introduction: what you do now and why property.
  2. We meet for a coffee chat. You ask anything, and we both decide if it fits.
  3. New to the industry? Registration as a Real Estate Negotiator (REN) is done through the agency.
